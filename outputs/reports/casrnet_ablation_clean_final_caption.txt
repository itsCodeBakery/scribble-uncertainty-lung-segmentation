Ablation study of the proposed CASR-Net framework. Scribble-only supervision performs poorly due to sparse lesion annotations. Adding background guidance improves segmentation, while weak uncertainty refinement provides a small additional gain. The complete CASR-Net framework achieves the best performance by combining scribble anchors, background constraints, teacher-guided pseudo-labeling, confidence-aware refinement, contextual 2.5D input, and TTA-based inference.
