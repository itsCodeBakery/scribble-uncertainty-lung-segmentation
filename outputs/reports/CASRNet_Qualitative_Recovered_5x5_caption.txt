Qualitative prediction results using the recovered CASR-Net student checkpoint. Each row shows a representative chest CT slice, ground-truth infection mask, CASR-Net prediction, boundary overlay, and error map. In the boundary overlay, green denotes the ground-truth boundary, red denotes the predicted boundary, and yellow denotes overlapping boundaries. In the error map, white denotes true positive pixels, red denotes false positive pixels, and blue denotes false negative pixels.
